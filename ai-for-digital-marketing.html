Where AI helps most

AI can reduce repetitive work, but it should support your judgment rather than replace it. Good use cases include brainstorming, content outlines, summarization of your own research, idea clustering and drafting variations.

A simple workflow

  1. Define the audience and goal.
  2. Collect reliable source information.
  3. Ask AI for an outline or alternatives.
  4. Fact-check important claims.
  5. Add your own examples and experience.
  6. Edit for clarity and publish only when useful to the reader.

Use AI to improve—not multiply—content

Publishing dozens of generic AI pages can create a poor experience. A smaller number of genuinely useful articles with original insight is a stronger foundation for a long-term website.

Practical examples

  • Create a content calendar from a defined niche.
  • Turn one long article into social post ideas.
  • Group Search Console queries into content themes.
  • Generate FAQ ideas that you then verify and answer.
