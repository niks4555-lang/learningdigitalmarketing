30-Day Digital Marketing Plan for Beginners

Updated August 2026 • Practical beginner guide

In this guide
  • What it means
  • How to get started
  • Practical tips
  • Common mistakes

Week 1: Build the foundation

  • Define one audience.
  • Set up Search Console and analytics.
  • Create your main categories.
  • Publish 2–3 genuinely useful beginner guides.

Week 2: Build content depth

  • Publish supporting articles around your core topics.
  • Add internal links.
  • Improve titles and introductions.
  • Create simple social posts from your articles.

Week 3: Test monetization

  • Research relevant affiliate programs.
  • Write one useful comparison or review.
  • Add a clear affiliate disclosure.
  • Measure clicks instead of assuming revenue.

Week 4: Measure and improve

  • Review search impressions and clicks.
  • Identify pages with impressions but low click-through rate.
  • Improve pages that already show demand.
  • Plan next month's content from actual data.

The goal

The first 30 days are about building a repeatable system, not expecting guaranteed income. Consistency and useful content are the assets you are building.
