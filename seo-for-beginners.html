SEO for Beginners: Where Should You Start?

Updated August 2026 • Practical beginner guide

In this guide
  • What it means
  • How to get started
  • Practical tips
  • Common mistakes

What is SEO?

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a website so that search engines can understand its content and users can discover it through relevant searches. SEO is not a shortcut to instant traffic; it is a long-term process of creating useful pages and making them easy to crawl, understand and navigate.

Start with search intent

Before choosing a keyword, ask what the searcher actually wants. A query can be informational, commercial, navigational or transactional. Your page should satisfy that intent instead of forcing a sales pitch into an informational query.

Choose focused topics

For a new website, start with specific questions and long-tail topics that match your audience. Build related articles around a broader subject and connect them with useful internal links.

Improve the page itself

  • Write a clear, descriptive title.
  • Use headings that help readers scan the page.
  • Answer the main question early.
  • Add original examples, screenshots or observations where possible.
  • Link to relevant pages on your own site.

Common SEO mistakes

Avoid keyword stuffing, copied content, misleading titles and publishing many thin pages simply to increase the page count. Focus on usefulness first and measurement second.
