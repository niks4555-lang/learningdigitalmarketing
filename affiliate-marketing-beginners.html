What is affiliate marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based model where a publisher recommends a product or service using a tracked link. If a qualifying action occurs, the publisher may receive a commission according to the program's terms.

Choose trust before commission

Pick products that genuinely fit your audience. A high commission is not useful if the product is irrelevant or if readers do not trust the recommendation.

Content that can work

  • Hands-on reviews
  • Feature comparisons
  • Beginner buying guides
  • Alternatives and use-case guides
  • Tutorials that naturally reference a tool

Disclose affiliate relationships

Be transparent when a page contains affiliate links. Clearly explain that you may earn a commission if the reader purchases through a qualifying link. Follow the requirements of each affiliate program and applicable advertising rules.

Do not promise guaranteed income

Affiliate results depend on traffic, niche, audience trust, conversion rates and program terms. Treat it as a business experiment that requires useful content and measurement.
