Do not buy tools before you have a problem to solve

Beginners often collect too many subscriptions. Start with free or low-cost tools, learn the workflow and upgrade only when a limitation is genuinely slowing you down.

A practical starter stack

  • Analytics for measuring visitors and behavior.
  • Search performance tools for queries and indexing.
  • A simple design tool for visual content.
  • A spreadsheet for content planning and calculations.
  • An AI assistant for brainstorming and organization.

Evaluate tools on five criteria

  1. Does it solve a real problem?
  2. Is the data reliable?
  3. Can you export your work?
  4. Does the price make sense for your current stage?
  5. Will you actually use it every week?
