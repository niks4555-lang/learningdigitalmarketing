Dropshipping: What Beginners Should Know

Updated August 2026 • Practical beginner guide

In this guide

  • Common mistakes

How dropshipping works

In a typical dropshipping model, a store markets products without holding the full inventory itself. When an order is placed, the supplier fulfills it under the agreed process. The store remains responsible for customer communication, policies and the overall buying experience.

Start with product validation

Do not begin by buying large quantities. Research demand, competition, shipping time, product quality, returns and the actual landed cost.

Calculate the real margin

Revenue is not profit. Consider product cost, shipping, payment fees, marketplace or platform fees, advertising, refunds, taxes and other operating costs before deciding whether a product is viable.

Protect the customer experience

  • Use clear shipping estimates.
  • Publish a return/refund policy.
  • Order samples when possible.
  • Respond to customer questions.
  • Do not make unsupported product claims.
